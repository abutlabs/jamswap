# Soak results, 2026-09-28

**Headline.** The mixed net (3 lasair + 3 PolkaJam) passes a full hour on lasair 2.1.2.
The all-lasair net (6 lasair) passes 25 minutes but fails an hour. Once the DEX's rounds
grow to 48 orders, lasair refines each one too slowly for its work-package to be
guaranteed in time. The chain itself was healthy in every run. What failed is how fast
lasair turns big work into guarantees.

## What we learned

1. **lasair 2.1.2 made the mixed net pass the hour.** On 2.1.1 the same hour had 17
   orders refused, for a reason we could not see: the load generator only logged "Bad
   Request", and its log went with the net. Soaks now keep `loadgen.log` and `dex.log`,
   and the load generator prints the DEX's reason. 2.1.2 carries lasair's memory fixes
   (a capped allocator, periodic heap compaction, at most two co-guarantee refines at
   once). With them the hour passed with 0 refused and lower latency.

2. **The all-lasair net fails after about 25 minutes, and we know why.** The run on the
   dashboards traced it:
   - The DEX's market-1 rounds grow to the 48-order cap. One such round is a 253M-gas
     work-package, and lasair refines it in 14–27 s ("Refine seconds" climbs from 2 s to
     12–15 s on the lasair dashboard).
   - A package arrives with about half of its anchor window already gone. The window
     is 8 blocks, about 30 s at this net's 3.6 s slots. The refine plus the next slot
     use the rest, and the package expires:
     `anchor_too_old`, 5 on one node in 20 minutes.
   - The round times out, its orders go back in line, the backlog grows, and the DEX
     starts refusing orders at each account's open-order cap: 387, then 519 refused.
   - On the mixed net PolkaJam's co-guarantors are fast enough, so the same load passes.

   The fix is lasair's refine speed. It's tracked in lasair (issue 80) and being worked
   on; the lasair dashboard's refine-time, anchor-age and packages-expired panels will
   show whether it worked.

3. **The chain side is solid.** Seven runs, about 2,900 finalized slots: never a second
   head, never a finality conflict, never a state mismatch between nodes or between
   lasair and PolkaJam.

## Reproduce

```
LASAIR_IMAGE=ghcr.io/abutlabs/lasair:2.1.2 LASAIR_DATA_DIR=/data soak/run lasair-pj 3600   # passes
LASAIR_IMAGE=ghcr.io/abutlabs/lasair:2.1.2 LASAIR_DATA_DIR=/data soak/run lasair6 3600     # fails at ~25 min (lasair issue 80)
LASAIR_IMAGE=ghcr.io/abutlabs/lasair:2.1.2 LASAIR_DATA_DIR=/data soak/run lasair6 1500     # passes
```
